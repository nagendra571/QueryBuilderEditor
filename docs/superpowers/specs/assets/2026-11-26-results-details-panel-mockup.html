Results grid, phase 2: the side panel

Both options give you a record panel, a column profile (computed from the loaded rows, honest about cut-off) and selection totals in the footer. They differ in how the panel opens and what it shows. Click the one that fits how your users work; tell me what to change in the terminal.

A

Inspector that follows you

A "Details" button opens a docked panel with two tabs, Row and Column. Both follow the cell you're on: arrow down and the Row tab shows the next record; move right and the Column tab profiles that column. Like Snowflake's inspector. Best for exploring.

B

Open on demand

Nothing extra in the toolbar. You open a record from the row-number cell ("Open record", or Space on a selected row) and a profile from the column menu ("Profile this column"). The panel shows that one thing until you close it or pick another; it does not follow the cursor. Calmer; fewer surprises.
