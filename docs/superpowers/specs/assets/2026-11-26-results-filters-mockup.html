3. Filtering from the Details panel

The Row tab gets a small filter action per field on hover: "Show only this value".

How it behaves

Where filters live: a filter mark in every header (blue when active), the right-click menu on any cell, and the Details panel.
Text and Yes/No: a searchable checklist of the values in the loaded rows with counts, plus "(empty)". Numbers and dates: from/to with a small histogram, plus "Include empty values".
Combining: several values in one column = any of them; filters on different columns = all of them; search still narrows on top.
Chips: one per filtered column; ✕ removes it, clicking it reopens its filter; "Clear all". Footer reads "… of 412 (filtered from 1,000)".
Cut-off results: the chip bar says "Filtering the first 1,000 rows". Add to query turns the chips into builder filters (Totals filters for summary columns), clears them from the grid, and re-runs.
Totals and profile: footer totals and Copy follow the filtered rows; the Column profile describes the filtered rows and says so.
