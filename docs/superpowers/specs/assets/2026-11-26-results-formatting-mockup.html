Colours: red, amber, green, blue. Up to 5 rules per column.
Format tab follows the active column; ‹ › steps to the previous / next column. Header menu gains Format…, which opens this tab. A small blue dot in the header marks a formatted column.
Totals row (Σ Totals in the toolbar): one row stuck to the bottom. Click a totals cell to pick Sum, Average, Count, Min, Max or None. Numbers start on Sum, dates on Latest, text on Count (or Distinct).
Bars are drawn behind the number, sized from zero to the largest value in the loaded rows (after the grid's filters). Negatives grow left from a zero line.
Rules colour the cell only. Numbers: >, <, between, equals. Text: is, contains, is empty. Dates: before, after, between. Rules sit on top of a colour scale.
